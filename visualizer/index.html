Garages At A Glance

Run Types By Garage

Each garage's runs by duty type. Hover a part for its count.

Swings By Garage

Share of each garage's runs that are swings (a quality of a run, so a Regular, Block or Part Time run can be one).

Routes Served By Garage

Routes with at least one in-service trip from the garage; "shared" routes are also run by another garage then. Hover a bar for the list.

Where Trips Start From By Garage

How each in-service trip begins: straight out of the garage (pullout), after a deadhead on the same route, coming off another route, or turning around.

Service Efficiency By Garage

Share of each garage's platform time (pull-out to pull-in) spent carrying passengers, on a 0–100% scale. The last row is every garage together. Hover a bar for the hours.

How Buses Spend Their Time By Garage

Each garage's platform time (operators on a bus), split into carrying passengers, waiting (layovers and recovery), deadheading between routes, and driving to and from the garage. Service efficiency = share of platform time in service.

Top 10 Routes By Trip Origin

Top 10 Routes With The Most Short Trips

Trips that start or end short of a terminal (short lines and short turns), both directions together. A trip handed to another run mid-route is judged by where the bus finally ends up.

Top 10 Routes By Busiest Hour

The most departures a route has in one direction within a single clock hour, and the frequency that works out to.

Top 10 Most Efficient Routes

Share of buses' platform time spent in service. Layovers, deadheads and pullouts count toward the route of the next trip; the pull-in toward the last one. Routes with at least 10 trips in the period.
